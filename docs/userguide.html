Running a session

Open court-call.web.app in any browser. Nothing to install and no password for the everyday things — marking who's playing, making the pairings and logging winners all work straight away. The app nudges you toward the next step as you go.

Mark who's playing

  1. Open the app. You start on the Today tab.
  2. Set the date, the start time and how long you're playing.
  3. Tap a name to mark someone in. Tap again to take them out.
  4. Can't find someone? Use the search box, or Add someone new at the bottom.

The number at the top right counts everyone marked in. That number decides how many courts you'll use. Tap the ⓘ beside any name to see their ratings and record.

Make the pairings

  1. Tap Courts at the top.
  2. Choose what each set should be: Mixed, Split by sex, or Singles. It starts on mixed, split, mixed.
  3. Use the − and + buttons to change how many sets you're playing.
  4. Tap Generate Pairings.

The line under each set tells you what it will produce before you commit, based on who's marked in.

Share it and record the results

  1. On Results, tap Copy for GroupMe and paste it into the club chat.
  2. As each set finishes, tap the winning pair's name.
  3. Not happy with a set? Tap Adjust, then tap two players to swap them.
  4. Want a completely different draw? Tap Re-roll pairings.

Winners can be changed later if you tap the wrong one — just tap the correct pair instead. Logging winners is what builds everyone's record, so it's worth the habit.

Always check the real court calendar before you play. Court Call picks court numbers on its own and has no way of knowing about lessons or USTA matches. There's a link to the club's calendar at the top of the Courts tab. That check is on you, not the app.

Questions that come up

What does "split by sex" actually do?
That set puts the women on one court together and the men on another, wherever there are four of each. Anyone left over plays mixed as usual. If there aren't four of a sex, that side isn't split and the set runs normally.
What's the 🔥 next to a name?
Three or more wins in a row. The number after it is how many. There's a key at the bottom of the pairings sheet, so it shows up in screenshots too.
Someone's rating is wrong.
Tap Directory at the top right, enter your PIN, then the pencil icon next to their name. The CTA rating is the one pairing uses, and it takes any value — 3.7 is fine. USTA is only used for players without a CTA rating. Winning and losing never changes either.
How do I know what a 3 or a 4 means?
There's a What do these ratings mean? link right under the competitive and serving dropdowns. It explains all five levels of each.
Why a different number of courts than I expected?
Court count follows the headcount. Every four players make a doubles court, and a spare pair gets a singles court. Twenty-four is the most it can pair at once.
I made the pairings on my phone but I'm on my laptop now.
Each browser keeps its own sheet. On the Today tab, look for "other sessions in progress" and tap Take a copy. It brings across the latest version, hand adjustments included, and doesn't change the original.
Someone always plays on the same court.
That's a court preference set in their directory entry, honoured every set. You can change or remove it by editing that player.
Starting a new week — will I lose anything?
Start New Week clears who's playing and this week's pairings. The player list and every past result stay exactly where they are.
The app won't load, or won't take my PIN.
Almost always a browser blocking it. In Brave, tap the lion in the address bar and turn Shields off for this site, then refresh. Nothing is lost when this happens — the app just can't reach its data.
Can I see who won what?
The History tab shows every player's win/loss record and every logged match. Anyone can read it. Changing or deleting a result needs a PIN.
Something's wrong and I don't know who to tell.
Scroll to the bottom and tap Feedback or questions?. It goes straight to the organizer's inbox.
Tip — put it on your home screen. When your browser offers to add Court Call to your home screen, say yes. It then opens like an app, without the address bar, and works exactly the same.
If you have a PIN, two things to know. Importing a spreadsheet can change many players at once — the app shows you every change first, so read that screen before confirming. And it saves a snapshot beforehand, so a bad import can be undone.
